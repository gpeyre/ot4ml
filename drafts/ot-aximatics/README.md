# OT axiomatics

This draft records the precise convex-extension characterization of the
Kantorovich cost.

The three proposed properties do **not** characterize the transport cost
uniquely. They imply that any such functional lies below the Kantorovich cost.
The correct theorem is that the Kantorovich cost is the **largest** jointly
convex weak-star lower-semicontinuous extension of the pointwise cost on Dirac
pairs. The note includes:

- a compact barycentric proof on compact metric spaces;
- a two-point counterexample to uniqueness without maximality;
- the general measure-cone theorem of Savare and Sodini;
- the connection with Fenchel--Moreau duality.
